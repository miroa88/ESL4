🔄 Unit 8: The Passive Voice

📖 Think of passive voice like a movie camera! In active voice, the camera follows the actor (subject) who does the action. In passive voice, we flip the camera to focus on what's being done, not who's doing it. Sometimes the actor doesn't even appear in the scene!

Ակտիվ ձայնում (Active Voice) սուբյեկտը կատարում է գործողություն։ Պասիվ ձայնում (Passive Voice) մենք կենտրոնանում ենք գործողության վրա, ոչ թե գործողություն կատարողի վրա։ Օրինակ՝ "The book was written" (Գիրքը գրվեց) փոխարեն "He wrote the book" (Նա գրեց գիրքը)։

🎯 The Magic Formula

Հիմնական բանաձև՝ BE բայը + Անցյալի դերբայ (Past Participle). Սա միշտ պետք է հիշել!
ACTIVE: Subject + VERB + Object
⬇️
PASSIVE: Object + BE (conjugated) + PAST PARTICIPLE + (by + Subject)

📝 Step-by-Step Transformation | Քայլ առ քայլ փոփոխություն

Step 1: Identify the object in the active sentence → This becomes your new subject
Active: Airports implemented new security measures.
→ New security measures will be our new subject
Step 2: Choose the correct form of BE (must match the original tense!)
"implemented" is simple past → use "were"
→ New security measures were
Step 3: Change the main verb to its past participle form
implement → implemented
→ New security measures were implemented
Step 4: Optional: Add "by + original subject" (only if it's important info)
→ New security measures were implemented (by airports).

🎬 Visual Example - Watch the Transformation!

Active Voice Passive Voice
The teacher explains the lesson. The lesson is explained (by the teacher).
Scientists discovered a new species. A new species was discovered (by scientists).
People have used computers for decades. Computers have been used for decades.
Նկատեք՝ օբյեկտը (ինչ որ բանը, որի վրա գործողությունն է կատարվում) դառնում է սուբյեկտ։

Ներկա պարզ		 am/is/are + past participle English is spoken in many countries.

Անցյալ պարզ		 was/were + past participle The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci.
Մոնա Լիզան նկարվեց Լեոնարդո դա Վինչիի կողմից։

Ներկա կատարյալ		 has/have been + past participle The problem has been solved.

Past Perfect
Անցյալ կատարյալ		 had been + past participle The report had been finished before the meeting.

Ապառնի		 will be + past participle The results will be announced tomorrow.

Մոդալ բայեր		 modal + be + past participle This problem can be solved easily.

Մոդալ կատարյալ		 modal + have been + past participle The accident could have been prevented.

Դերբայական գոյական		 being + past participle Nobody likes being criticized.

🎯 When to Use Passive Voice - 4 Critical Situations

Չորս կարևոր իրավիճակներ, երբ պետք է օգտագործել պասիվ ձայնը

1️⃣ To Focus Attention on New/Important Information

Երբ ուզում ենք ուշադրություն հրավիրել նոր կամ կարևոր տեղեկության վրա

The Rule: Put the most important information at the beginning (subject position).

Barack Obama was first elected president in 2008.
→ We care about Obama, not about the millions of voters

The art world has long admired Banksy, a mysterious street artist. In spite of great efforts, Banksy's identity has not been confirmed.
→ Focus on Banksy's identity, not on "people" who tried to confirm it
This is called "information flow" - move from known (old) information to new information smoothly.

2️⃣ To Avoid Repetitive/Unnecessary Information

Երբ ակնհայտ է, թե ով է կատարել գործողությունը, ուստի կրկնելը ավելորդ է

The Rule: When it's obvious who did the action, don't mention them again.

Students were tested at the beginning and end of the semester. (We assume teachers were the ones who tested students.)

Male professors are much more likely to be described as "brilliant" in student evaluations. (We know students describe the professors because these are student evaluations.)

3️⃣ To Express Information Politely or Avoid Blame

Երբ ուզում ենք ավելի քաղաքավարի լինել կամ խուսափել ուղղակի մեղադրանքից

The Rule: Use passive to soften criticism or avoid pointing fingers.

The candidate was accused of spreading false rumors about his competition.
→ More diplomatic than "Someone accused the candidate..."

Students are not always given the option of retaking a test.
→ Avoids directly criticizing teachers

He apologized for remarks that were considered offensive.
→ Doesn't say who found them offensive

4️⃣ To Convey Objectivity and Impersonal Tone

Գիտական հոդվածներում անկողմնակալ և անձնազուրկ տոն ստեղծելու համար

The Rule: In scientific/academic writing, use passive to sound objective and professional.

Participants should be given as much information as possible about the research ahead of time.
→ Focuses on the procedure, not on "researchers"

Results were analyzed with a standard statistical method.
→ Emphasizes the scientific process
Fun Fact: About 30% of clauses in scientific articles use passive voice! This helps create that "objective science" tone.
Common Error 8.1: Using the Wrong Verb Form
Սխալ բայաձև օգտագործելը - Հիշեք՝ միշտ անցյալի դերբայ (Past Participle), ոչ թե պարզ անցյալ
❌ Several languages are spoke in Switzerland.
Wrong! "spoke" is simple past, NOT past participle
✅ Several languages are spoken in Switzerland.
Correct! "spoken" is the past participle of "speak"

Remember: The formula is BE + PAST PARTICIPLE (not simple past!)

Common Irregular Past Participles:

Base Simple Past Past Participle Example in Passive
write wrote written The book was written in 1999.
speak spoke spoken English is spoken here.
break broke broken The vase was broken.
choose chose chosen She was chosen as leader.
drive drove driven The car is driven daily.
Common Error 8.2: Forgetting "BE"
BE բայի բացակայությունը - Հիշեք՝ պասիվում ՄԻՇՏ պետք է լինի BE բայ
❌ Mo Yan interviewed after winning the Nobel Prize.
Missing "was"! This is incomplete
✅ Mo Yan was interviewed after winning the Nobel Prize.
Complete! Has BE (was) + past participle (interviewed)

Key Rule: Passive voice MUST have: BE verb + past participle

Without BE, it's not passive - it's nothing! ❌

Common Error 8.3: Using Passive with Intransitive Verbs
Պասիվը միայն անցողական բայերի հետ - Անցողական բայերը կարող են ունենալ ուղղակի խնդիր
❌ 100 million people were died in the pandemic.
Wrong! "Die" cannot take an object, so no passive
✅ 100 million people died in the pandemic.
Correct! "Die" stays active

The Test: Can you answer "what?" or "whom?" after the verb?

  • ✅ "Build what?" → a house (transitive - CAN be passive)
  • ❌ "Happen what?" → nothing! (intransitive - CANNOT be passive)
Common intransitive verbs that CANNOT be passive: die, happen, occur, exist, appear, arrive, come, go, rise, fall, sleep

🎨 Unit 10: Adjective Clauses (Relative Clauses)

📖 Think of adjective clauses as detective clues! They give us extra information to identify someone or something. They answer: "Which one?" or "What kind?" Just like a detective describes a suspect!

Ածական ստորադասյալ նախադասություններ (Adjective Clauses) նկարագրում և ճշտում են գոյականները։ Սկսվում են հարաբերական դերանուններով (relative pronouns)՝ that, who, whom, which, whose

🎯 What Are Adjective Clauses?

An adjective clause (also called a relative clause) modifies a noun or noun phrase. It contains a subject and verb, and begins with a relative pronoun.

Noun + RELATIVE PRONOUN + Subject + Verb
Simple sentence: Transpiration is a process.
With adjective clause: Transpiration is a process that involves plants.
↑ The clause describes "process"
Թրանսպիրացիան գործընթաց է, որը վերաբերում է բույսերին։

🔑 Choosing the Right Relative Pronoun

Ճիշտ հարաբերական դերանուն ընտրելը՝ կախված է մարդկանց կամ իրերի համար, սուբյեկտ կամ օբյեկտ դիրքում
Use For Position Pronoun Example
People
Մարդիկ		 Subject who or that Jane Austen is the author who wrote Emma.
People
Մարդիկ		 Object whom or that Rosa Parks is the activist whom the police arrested.
Things
Իրեր		 Subject that or which Peru is a country that is located in South America.
Things
Իրեր		 Object that or which Peru is a country that millions visit each year.
Possession
Պատկանելիություն		 Any whose The student whose book I borrowed is here.
In academic writing, use whom instead of who for objects - it sounds more formal and professional!

📊 Restrictive vs. Nonrestrictive Clauses

Կարևոր տարբերություն՝ սահմանափակող (restrictive) և ոչ սահմանափակող (nonrestrictive) նախադասություններ

1️⃣ Restrictive Clauses (NO commas) - Essential Information

Purpose: Identifies WHICH ONE we're talking about - essential to meaning

The ice cream flavor that is ordered most often is vanilla.
→ Tells us WHICH flavor (essential information)
Պաղպաղակի համը, որը ամենից հաճախ են պատվիրում, վանիլն է։

Rule: NO commas, can use "that" or "which"

2️⃣ Nonrestrictive Clauses (USE commas) - Extra Information

Purpose: Adds extra detail but not essential - we already know which one

Vanilla is more popular than chocolate, which is people's number two choice.
→ Adds extra info about chocolate (not essential)
Վանիլը ավելի հանրաճանաչ է, քան շոկոլադը, որը երկրորդ տեղում է մարդկանց ընտրություններում։

Rule: USE commas, NEVER use "that" (only "which" for things, "who" for people)

❌ Vanilla is popular, that is my favorite.
Wrong! Can't use "that" after comma
✅ Vanilla is popular, which is my favorite.
Correct! Use "which" after comma

✂️ Reduced Adjective Clauses

Կարճացված ածական նախադասություններ՝ ավելի հակիրճ դարձնելու համար

Method 1: With verb "be" - Delete pronoun and "be"

Original: The fish that is upsetting the local ecosystem...
Reduced: The fish upsetting the local ecosystem...
Original: The fish that is known as the snakehead fish...
Reduced: The fish known as the snakehead fish...

Method 2: With other verbs - Delete pronoun, add -ing

Original: Anyone who wants to be a pilot...
Reduced: Anyone wanting to be a pilot...
Original: Students who study hard...
Reduced: Students studying hard...
Important: You can only reduce when the relative pronoun is the SUBJECT of the clause!
Common Error 10.1: Forgetting the relative pronoun
Հարաբերական դերանուն մոռանալը սուբյեկտ դիրքում
❌ A university major Ø deals with business is likely to result in higher earnings.
Missing "that"!
✅ A university major that deals with business is likely to result in higher earnings.
Correct with "that"
Common Error 10.2: Using wrong pronoun for things
Սխալ դերանուն օգտագործելը իրերի համար
❌ California, that was the most populous state...
Wrong pronoun!
✅ California, which was the most populous state...
Use "which" for things

⏰ Unit 11: Adverb Clauses

📖 Adverb clauses are the "why, when, and how" storytellers! They explain circumstances: When did it happen? Why? Under what conditions?

Մակբայական ստորադասյալ նախադասություններ (Adverb Clauses) բացատրում են ՝ երբ, ինչու, ինչպես։ Սկսվում են ստորադասական շաղկապներով (subordinating conjunctions)

🎭 Types of Adverb Clauses

Type Conjunctions Example
Time
Ժամանակ		 when, while, before, after, until, as soon as, once When people are stressed, they exhibit symptoms.
Reason
Պատճառ		 because, since Because his father left, his mother supported the family.
Contrast
Հակադրություն		 while, whereas While his mother's career wasn't prosperous, Chaplin had success.
Concession
Զիջում		 although, though, even though Even though Chaplin was difficult, his films were successful.
Purpose
Նպատակ		 so that, in order that Chaplin visited so that he could accept an award.
Comma Rule: Adverb clause FIRST = use comma. Adverb clause SECOND = usually no comma!
✅ After the game ended, we went home.
✅ We went home after the game ended.

✂️ Reducing Adverb Clauses

Կարճացված մակբայական նախադասություններ (երբ սուբյեկտները նույնն են)

With "be" - Delete subject and "be"

When people are stressed → When stressed, people...

With other verbs - Delete subject, add -ing

While people sleep → While sleeping, people...

Reason clauses - Delete all, use -ing

Because the students had read → Having read the manual...
Common Error 11.1: Wrong conjunction
Սխալ շաղկապ օգտագործելը
❌ Communication failures happen although people misunderstand.
Wrong meaning!
✅ Communication failures happen because people misunderstand.
Shows reason correctly
Common Error 11.2: Comma splice
Ստորակետով սխալ միացում՝ պետք է շաղկապ օգտագործել
❌ The student is immersed in the language, he learns quickly.
Comma splice! Need conjunction
✅ When the student is immersed, he learns quickly.
Correct with conjunction

💭 Unit 12: Noun Clauses

📖 Noun clauses are like containers! They hold complete thoughts and work like nouns - as subjects, objects, or after "be".

Գոյականային ստորադասյալ նախադասություններ (Noun Clauses) աշխատում են ինչպես գոյականներ։ Կարող են լինել սուբյեկտ, օբյեկտ կամ լինել BE բայից հետո։

🎯 Types of Noun Clauses

1️⃣ "That" Clauses (from statements)

Scientists know that the earth is getting warmer.
Գիտնականները գիտեն, որ Երկիրը ավելի տաք է դառնում։

2️⃣ Wh- Clauses (from questions)

The report explains how the banks were rescued.
Զեկույցը բացատրում է, թե ինչպես փրկվեցին բանկերը։

3️⃣ If/Whether Clauses (from yes/no questions)

They wanted to know if the experiment was successful.
Նրանք ուզում էին իմանալ, արդյոք փորձը հաջողվեց։
In noun clauses, use STATEMENT word order, NOT question order!
❌ I wonder how can we save languages.
✅ I wonder how we can save languages.

🗣️ Reported Speech (Indirect Speech)

Անուղղակի խոսք - ինչպես փոխանցել ուրիշի խոսքը
Direct Speech Indirect Speech
"I am going" She said she was going
"Interest rates are stable" They said interest rates were stable
"I will help" He said he would help
"I can do it" She said she could do it
Common Error 12.1: Question word order
Հարցական կարգ օգտագործելը գոյականային նախադասություններում
❌ Linguists wonder how can we save languages.
Question order - wrong!
✅ Linguists wonder how we can save languages.
Statement order - correct!
Common Error 12.2: That vs. What
"That" և "What" շփոթելը
❌ The paper didn't report what the researchers discovered.
Use "that" for statements!
✅ The paper didn't report that the researchers discovered something.
"That" introduces a statement
